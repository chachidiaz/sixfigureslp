## The one freebie worth making first

Make a one-page handout that answers the question parents ask you most. For a lot of pediatric SLPs, that’s <mark>“Is my child’s speech on track?”</mark>

> A good freebie solves one small problem today and makes the bigger problem feel solvable with you.
