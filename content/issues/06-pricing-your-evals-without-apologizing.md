An evaluation isn’t one hour of your time. It’s the scheduling, the testing, the scoring, the report and the parent meeting. Price the whole thing.

## Count every hour

1. List each step from first call to final report.
2. Add up the real time, not the time you wish it took.
3. Multiply by the hourly rate you need to hit your income goal.

> Your rate is information, not an apology. Say it, then stop talking.

## Practice the sentence

Say your price out loud until it sounds boring. <mark>Boring is confident.</mark>
