The families you want are already talking to someone you could be friends with: their pediatrician, their preschool teacher, their OT.

## Who to call first

1. Pediatric offices within fifteen minutes of you.
2. Preschools and daycares nearby.
3. OTs and PTs who don’t offer speech.

## What to say

> “Hi, I’m a local speech therapist. I’d love to drop off a few referral cards and learn what your families ask about most.”

Lead with curiosity. <mark>Ask what they need before you say what you do.</mark>
