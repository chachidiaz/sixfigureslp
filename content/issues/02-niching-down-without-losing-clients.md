Niching down feels like closing doors. In practice, it puts a big sign over the one door the right families are looking for.

## Specific is easier to remember

“I help kids who stutter” is something a pediatrician can repeat. “I do speech therapy” isn’t.

> You can still see anyone you want. Your marketing just talks to one family at a time.

## Try it this week

Rewrite your bio’s first sentence to <mark>name one kind of client you love working with</mark>.
