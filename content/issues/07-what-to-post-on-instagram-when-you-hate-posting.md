Most SLPs we talk to don’t hate Instagram. They hate staring at a blank caption box on Sunday night. The fix isn’t more ideas, it’s fewer decisions.

## The three-post week

- **Monday:** one quick tip parents can use at dinner tonight.
- **Wednesday:** a behind-the-scenes look at your therapy room.
- **Friday:** a client win, shared with permission.

> Consistency beats creativity. A plain post every Monday does more than a perfect post every month.

## Batch it once

Block one hour a month to <mark>write all twelve captions in a single sitting</mark>. Schedule them and close the app.
