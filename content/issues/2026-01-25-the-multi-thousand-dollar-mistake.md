I’ve talked to a lot of SLPs who are transitioning to more online offers. If there is one thing I can identify that weaves them all together it’s this: value proposition.

Value proposition is the first thing you’ll learn in any formal marketing program. It’s Marketing 101, yet it’s the most overlooked and miscalculated concept in the game.

In both the $3M companies I’ve worked for and $20M companies I’ve worked for, each of them miscalculated the positioning and/or the value proposition and it cost THOOOUUSSSAANNNDDDS.

Okay so let’s get down to it: what the eff is value proposition?

## The Barebones Definition

A value proposition is the specific promise of value you deliver. It’s the ‘why’ behind someone choosing you over a competitor over staying stuck. <mark>Your solution should be in alignment and speaking to the values of your audience.</mark>

## How they Get it Wrong

What happens 10/10 times is entrepreneurs will over value their own perspective. They believe that what they’re offering has value for the reasons they’ve determined and therefore they end up with a misaligned product and/or misaligned positioning. When that happens, you create marketing language that doesn’t solve problems or doesn’t resonate with the people it’s supposedly for.

For example, for a recent company I worked for, we created a membership product because we assumed that’s what people wanted and memberships were all the rage. It was also suggested to us by a product guru.

A BIG part of the marketing around this membership product was the “community” aspect and interacting with and engaging with people in similar situations. We positioned the solution to their problem as a community.

In addition, one of the major purchasing frictions and hesitations for our audience was affordability and ease of access. The product had to be affordable and had to be really easy to access.

So what did we do? We jacked up the price and buried it inside a clunky course platform they never logged into. 🫠

Our audience told us through surveys (and through not buying it) that it was too expensive, and they don’t want another community to access online, and all they really valued was affordability, credibility, and ease of access.

They also just wanted the courses.<br>
Not the community.<br>
Not the live events.<br>
None of the additional stuff that required more time, more online presence, or just more in general.

So we had to change the value proposition.

Value prop went from: “this is the community we wanted when we started” or “this is an experience you need that’ll transform you”

To: “this is an all-access-pass to the entire course library for one low cost, created by an expert, self-paced and accessed through an app”

## Let’s do an Exercise

Ready to determine your value proposition?

Let’s do it!

### Step 1: ICA

To determine the value proposition, you need to first determine your ideal client avatar and figure out their very specific pain points. If you don’t have an audience yet or don’t feel comfortable asking your people then put this into ChatGPT with this prompt:

```text
I own a _________ business. I serve SLPs trying to ______________. I'm trying to determine an ideal client avatar. I need you to act as an expert marketer. For my business, generate the ideal client based on these factors:

Yearly Income
Education Level
Male or Female
Age Range
Geographic Location
Political Affiliation
Brand They Likely Shop
Music they likely listen to
What are their four to five major pain points?
What are their goals and aspirations?
What are their barriers to purchase?
What are 7-8 factors that would convince them to purchase from me?
Where do they get their information?
What does a day in the life look like?
```

### Step 2: Write down the Pain Points and Greatest Desires

This is key. Your pain points and desires from your ICA determines what specifically you will use to determine what YOUR IDEAL CLIENT VALUES (again, not what you value) when making a purchasing decision and if you solve their unique pain points.

Here’s an example from The Six Figure SLP when I asked what would convince my ideal clients to purchase from me:

1. **Direct Authority (The “No-Nonsense” Factor).** She is exhausted. She doesn’t want a “bestie” who tells her to “follow her heart.” She wants an expert who says, “Stop doing X, it’s wasting your time. Start doing Y, it’s where the money is.”
2. **Proof of “Clinical Exit” (The Path is Possible).** She needs to see that Emily has actually done it. Use “The Swing”—lead with the clinical credentials (the CCC-SLPs) but pivot to the revenue stats. If she sees a peer who successfully traded the “windowless closet” for a home office, the possibility becomes real.
3. **The “Niche Nuance” (Industry Specificity).** She has seen a thousand “business coaches” on Instagram, but she thinks they don’t understand the SLP world. You win when you mention HIPAA compliance, ASHA ethics, or the specific grind of IEP season. She needs to know your strategies work for therapists, not just life coaches or fitness influencers.

So, you’ll hear every reel, carousel, landing page, etc always mention our credentials first and foremost. We are a digital marketer and SLP duo - that’s what makes us different and we lead with that because that’s what you value.

(Not because ChatGPT told us so but also because through countless conversations)

### Step 3: Create your value proposition and positioning

Type this into ChatGPT:

```text
Write a value proposition based on the [insert the top three to four most accurate factors that would convince someone to Purchase from you]
```

For The Six Figure SLP:

> We teach veteran SLPs the high-level marketing frameworks and digital product strategies required to replace their clinical income with scalable online assets and make their first $100k in digital products.
>
> Most therapists are trapped in a “clinical cage,” trading 40+ hours a week for a fixed salary because no one taught them the business side of their degree. We bridge that gap. We don’t just “give advice”—we provide a rigorous, SLP-specific education on Email Marketing, Sales Funnels, and Digital Product Creation.
>
> By combining Emily’s “clinical-exit” blueprint with Chachi’s decade of $1M+ marketing expertise, we empower you to move from confused clinician to confident CEO. We provide the curriculum; you build the freedom.
>
> Stop “Googling” your business. Learn the systems that turn clinical expertise into a six-figure digital brand.

See how that works?

> It sounds basic, but 90% of entrepreneurs get it wrong because they fall in love with their own ideas instead of their customers’ problems.

As long as you’re always looking at your business and what you offer through the lens of what your audience finds important, you’ll be able to use the language and message that resonates with them.

Just never lose sight of their problems.

Talk to your audience, keep a pulse on what they need, and you’ll always have your value proposition in mind.
